# Research Analyze

1. Read `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` and find the latest run with status `RESULTS_READY`.
2. Read `00_MANIFEST.yaml`, `01_PLAN.md`, `02_RESULTS_RAW.md`, and relevant `raw/*` artifacts.
3. Write `03_ANALYSIS.md`: observations, outcome-branch mapping, supported/unsupported claims, remaining uncertainty, and at most one decisive next step when warranted.
4. Separately write `04_CRITIQUE.md`: strongest validity concern, leakage/confounds, statistical weakness, alternative explanation, overclaiming, and smallest material follow-up.
5. Keep observed facts distinct from inference. Never alter raw files to fit the analysis.
6. If Research Ponytail is enabled, use it to minimize unnecessary branches while preserving rigor.
7. Validate and advance the manifest states after the artifacts exist.
