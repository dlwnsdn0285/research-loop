# Research Analyze

Follow `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. Find the latest `RESULTS_READY` run and read its manifest, frozen plan, raw summary, and relevant raw artifacts. Write `03_ANALYSIS.md` by separating observations from inference and mapping results to predefined outcome branches. Then write a separate `04_CRITIQUE.md` focused on the strongest validity concern, confounds, alternative explanation, overclaiming, and the smallest follow-up that could materially change the conclusion. Never edit raw artifacts to fit the interpretation. If Research Ponytail is enabled, apply it only to reasoning and next-step selection.
